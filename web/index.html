Per-app figures come from Windows’ own GPU Engine counters (per-process GPU busy time) and per-process CPU time. The GPU watts above the card’s idle floor and the CPU watts above idle are split between processes in proportion to those two numbers. Idle baseline is what the machine burns just being switched on — PSU loss, board, fans, RAM, GPU idle floor — and is never charged to an app, so app shares are honest rather than inflated to fill the wall.
